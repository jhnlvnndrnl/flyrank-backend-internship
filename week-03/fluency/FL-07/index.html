Visual Identity

Personal Brand Identity

A clean, modern, and professional visual identity designed specifically for a software engineering portfolio. The system emphasizes clear structure, project content, and technical writing without distracting elements.

Style Note

Fonts: Syne for headings and Inter for body text. Colors: #2563EB, #111827, #F9FAFB, with #10B981 as the only accent.

Mood: Clean, technical, and confident. The visual identity should frame the work and allow projects, case studies, and engineering experience to be the primary focus.

A highly curated color scheme featuring high contrast, strict utility, and zero gradients. Use Emerald strictly for accents or success cues.
